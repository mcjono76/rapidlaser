About Rapid Laser

Engineering experience since 1986.

Rapid Laser is a division of Rapid Machining and Sheet Metal Fabrication, providing precision engineering and fabrication solutions from our Bayswater, Melbourne facility.

From small beginnings in 1986, Rapid Machining has continuously developed and is now a well respected, one-stop engineering business.

  • Established manufacturing experience Over 35 years in the industry
  • Precision equipment and skilled operators Delivering consistent, high-quality results
  • Design through to finished manufacture From your ideas to reality
